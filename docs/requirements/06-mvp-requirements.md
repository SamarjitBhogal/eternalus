# MVP requirements

Purpose: Read this when you need the definitive, testable list of what the Eternalus MVP must do
and how well it must do it, or when a task brief, PR, test name or ADR cites an `FR-`/`NFR-` id.
Every bullet of the product owner's requirements document and every "MVP decision" of the game
document is normalised here into one row with a stable id, a source, a status, a one-line
acceptance criterion and the phase that delivers it. The reference summary in
[`../reference/game-design/11-mvp-scope.md`](../reference/game-design/11-mvp-scope.md) gives the
yes/no on scope; this catalogue is the binding version with ids and traceability. Precedence
and the change procedure are in [`README.md`](README.md): a requirement changes only through an
ADR plus an edit here.

## How to read the tables

- **Source.** `Req:` cites a section of
  [`source/requirements-of-architecture.md`](source/requirements-of-architecture.md) (the
  requirements document); `Game:` cites a section of
  [`../reference/source/game-project-eternalus-v2.md`](../reference/source/game-project-eternalus-v2.md)
  (the game design document, Sep 18, 2026). `D<n>` cites a Phase 0 decision recorded in
  [`../decisions/`](../decisions/) (see [`README.md`](README.md)); `DISC-nn` cites
  [`../reference/suggestions/discrepancies.md`](../reference/suggestions/discrepancies.md).
- **Status.** **MVP** (built), **MVP-if-time** (built if the schedule allows; the seam is built
  regardless), **post-MVP** (extension point only) or **removed**. `cut n` is the item's place
  in the cut order of [`../plan/README.md` §4](../plan/README.md#4-mvp-cut-lines); a cut is a
  product-owner decision, not an agent's.
- **Acceptance.** One testable sentence. Numbers quoted from the sources are `TUNE` values that
  live in `GameConfig` or `content/` (CP-09); they are quoted so the test can be written, not
  because they are final. Anything the sources do not say is labelled `Recommendation:`.
- **Phase.** P1 kernel & world, P2 structures & combat, P3 minions/hordes/AI, P4
  Legends/abilities/loadouts, P5 meta UI & persistence, P6 hardening; `backlog` for post-MVP
  rows ([`../plan/backlog-post-mvp.md`](../plan/backlog-post-mvp.md)). Sprint numbers refer to
  the phase docs under [`../plan/`](../plan/).
- Ids are never reused or renumbered; a withdrawn row keeps its id with status **removed**.
  `FR-017`, `FR-025` and `FR-078` are already cited by other requirement docs.

## Coverage note

Every bullet of the requirements document's MVP list, developer-menu list, testing list,
hardcoding, map/minimap, settings, abilities and inheritance sections, and every "MVP decision"
and "included in MVP if possible" item of the game document, maps to a row above. The following
source items are deliberately **not** catalogued as requirements, with the reason:

- `Req: WHAT I NEED FROM YOU` (the plan, architecture, reference, requirements and research
  folders, the harness scaffold): these are the Phase 0 deliverables, tracked as the checklist
  of [`../plan/phase-0-documentation-and-harness.md`](../plan/phase-0-documentation-and-harness.md),
  not as product requirements; their standing rules are NFR-009, NFR-031 and NFR-032.
- `Req: Long term goal` "Everything in the reference document + much more": not mappable to a
  testable statement; the reference folder and the backlog hold it.
- `Req: Requirements of architecture` "TypeScript is allowed only where it is needed for OOP":
  superseded by D1 (full TypeScript), recorded in NFR-033 and `DISC-01`.
- `Game: Capture zones and mini-bosses` "Up until the mini-bosses spawn, the capture zones are
  protected by:" is unfinished in the source; FR-031 covers the zones without that guard rule,
  and the gap is listed in [`../reference/game-design/12-open-questions.md`](../reference/game-design/12-open-questions.md).
- `Game: Buildings` "holds the summoner (to be defined)" and `Game: Capture zones and
mini-bosses` "the player who lands the final hit gets a consumable item (keep or remove?)":
  undefined or open in the source (`DISC-43`); no row until the product owner decides.
- `Game: Default interactives` parry rules against Grasp and the "gravity exists" drop: they
  depend on the Flying class and are post-MVP with FR-056.
- `Game: Lore`, `Game: Art Theme` narrative and the "Team concern" / next-steps paragraphs: not
  requirements; the lore is reference material, the team walkthrough is Phase 0 sprint 0.5.
- `Game: Open Questions and Naming To-Dos`: naming items (rename Warrior and Flying, the bow,
  Studious, Death Ray, the second Sentinel artifact, the god in Fury) are tracked in
  [`../reference/suggestions/naming.md`](../reference/suggestions/naming.md) and shown as
  "(rename pending)" in the rows above; design questions stay in `12-open-questions.md`.
